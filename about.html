A continuous answer to Gujarat's tyre waste problem

Who we are

Garvim Energy LLP is a tyre pyrolysis company based at Vadharva, on the Malia–Ahmedabad Highway near Morbi, Gujarat. We operate a continuous tyre pyrolysis plant that converts end-of-life tyres into pyrolysis oil and carbon char, giving waste rubber a second life instead of letting it sit in landfills or burn in the open.

Our mission

To provide a safe, continuous, and environmentally compliant solution to Gujarat's growing tyre-waste problem — recovering value from every tyre we process, and reducing the pressure waste rubber puts on land, water, and air.

What makes our plant different

Continuous, safe, and built for compliance

Continuous, not batch

The plant runs non-stop rather than in small daily batches, so output is steady and predictable.

Built for safety

Negative-pressure reactor, nitrogen purging, dual mechanical seals, and SCADA monitoring throughout.

Designed for compliance

Emission control systems built to meet pollution control board standards.

Location & facility

In the heart of Gujarat's industrial belt

Our facility is located at Vadharva, on the Malia–Ahmedabad Highway, opposite Simpolo Ceramic, Morbi — with easy road access for both incoming tyre feedstock and outbound oil/char shipments.
